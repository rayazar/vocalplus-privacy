What you can do

Read web pages aloud

Open any page in the built-in browser and tap one button. Free.

Follow along

Every sentence is highlighted as you listen. Tap any sentence to jump to it.

Choose your voice

Pick from the voices on your phone, in different accents, and hear a sample first.

PDFs and ebooks

PDFs (including scanned ones), EPUB ebooks and Word documents. Pro.

Scan a page

Take a photo of printed text, or choose a picture, and have it read to you. Pro.

Emails and texts

Paste a message, or share it to VocalPlus from another app. Pro.
