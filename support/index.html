Common questions

How do I read a web page aloud?

Open the Browse tab, type or paste a web address, tap Go, then tap "Read this page aloud". VocalPlus switches to the Listening tab and starts reading.

What is included in Pro?

VocalPlus Pro unlocks PDFs (including scanned PDFs), EPUB ebooks, Word documents, photos of pages, and emails and texts that you paste or share into the app. Reading web pages is free.

How do I subscribe, and how much does it cost?

Open the Pro tab and choose a plan: $4.99 per month or $29.99 per year (prices may vary by country). Payment is handled by the App Store or Google Play.

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new phone?

Install VocalPlus, open the Pro tab, and tap "Restore purchases". Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account that you used to buy it.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose VocalPlus, and tap Cancel Subscription. Android: open the Google Play app, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, choose VocalPlus, and tap Cancel. We cannot cancel for you, because the App Store and Google Play handle billing.

Why can't I open my Kindle or Apple Books ebook?

Ebooks bought from stores are usually protected by DRM, and VocalPlus cannot open protected files. It works with DRM-free EPUB files, such as books from Project Gutenberg or files you own without protection.

My photo is not read well. How can I get better results?

Use good, even light, hold the phone steady and parallel to the page, fill the frame with the text, and avoid glare and shadows. Printed text works best. Handwriting and curved book pages are harder.

How do I get more natural voices?

VocalPlus uses the voices on your phone. On iPhone, open Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content, Voices, English, and download a voice marked Enhanced or Premium. On Android, open Settings, System, Languages, Text-to-speech output, Google, and install more voice data. New voices then appear in the VocalPlus voice list.

Does the app upload my documents or photos?

No. Documents and photos are read on your device and are not uploaded to our servers. Some voices on Android may use the internet to produce speech, which your phone's speech provider handles. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Everything the app saves (recent items and your voice choice) stays on your phone. Delete the app or clear its data to remove it. For purchase records, contact us at info@homula.com.
